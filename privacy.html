Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Who we are

Five Star Clips ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Five Star Clips channels. It is used only by its owner to publish short videos to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X.

What data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: with the owner's consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel and video statistics (youtube.readonly).
  • TikTok, Instagram, X: the app uses the official APIs to upload videos and read basic statistics of the owner's own accounts.

The app does not access, collect or store personal data of any other users or viewers.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer in a local configuration file. They are never shared, sold, or transferred to third parties, and are used only to perform the actions described above.

Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions or in the security settings of TikTok, Instagram and X.
